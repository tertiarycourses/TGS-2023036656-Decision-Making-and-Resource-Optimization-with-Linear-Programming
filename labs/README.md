## Topics

- **Topic 1 — Linear Programming** (Labs 1-3): formulation, Excel Solver max-profit, sensitivity & scenario analysis.
- **Topic 2 — Decision Analysis** (Labs 4-5): Expected Value / EVwPI / EVPI, risk & Expected Utility.
- **Topic 3 — Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis** (Labs 6-8): scoring board, AHP & consistency, integrated evaluation & KPIs.
